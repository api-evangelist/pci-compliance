Every year on 14 October, World Standards Day recognizes the people and organizations working together to develop standards that make our world safer, more connected, and more resilient. For the PCI Security Standards Council, this year’s celebration carries special significance: for 20 years, we have been setting payment security standards that help protect payment data around the globe.
