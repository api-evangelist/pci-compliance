The PCI Security Standards Council (PCI SSC) has published a new standard designed for entities involved in the use of cryptographic keys. The Payment Card Industry (PCI) Key Management and Operations (KMO)™ Standard v1.0 defines security requirements, test requirements, and guidance for entities involved in the operation and management of systems that use cryptographic keys for the security of account data.
