The PCI Security Standards Council (PCI SSC) has published a new information supplement addressing the security of artificial intelligence (AI) systems. This document covers both security aspects of using AI in payment environments and considerations when securing traditional systems against attacks that utilize AI systems. PCI SSC developed this document in collaboration with industry stakeholders including the Global Executive Assessor Roundtable (GEAR) and the Board of Advisors.
